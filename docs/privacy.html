Privacy Policy

Micro Rockets, by Tidal Forge Games. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Micro Rockets collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The app contains no networking code at all, and Tidal Forge Games operates no server for anything to be sent to.

What the app stores, and where

Micro Rockets keeps one thing on your device: your company. That is your cash and reputation, the rockets and ships you own, the jobs you have taken and flown, your bases and research, your records and achievements, which how-to cards you have read, and your settings. It is ordinary app storage, in the app's own container, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to Tidal Forge Games, because there is nowhere to send it. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier and no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking library. It is built with Skip's open-source frameworks, which hold no networking code either. It does not read your contacts, your photos, your location, your microphone or your music library, and it does not ask for permission to, because it never touches them.

Cloud save, which is the one exception

The app keeps a second copy of your company in your own iCloud, so a new phone picks up where the old one left off. This uses Apple's key-value storage, under your Apple Account, in a container only this app can read. It is your data in your iCloud: Tidal Forge Games cannot see it, has no key to it, and has no way to ask Apple for it.

It is on by default, and it is a switch. MENU › Settings › Cloud save turns it off, after which the company lives only on the device it is on. Turning it off does not remove the copy already in your iCloud; deleting the app from your devices and removing its data in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud does that.

Notifications

The app can tell you when a flight lands, when a ship docks and when research finishes. These are scheduled by the app on your phone and delivered by iOS. Nothing about them involves a server, and nothing about them leaves the device. All three are switches in Settings, and iOS will ask before the first one.

Purchases

Micro Rockets is a paid app with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Payment happens once, in the App Store, entirely between you and Apple. We never see your payment details and we receive no information that identifies you.

Apple's own services

Standard App Store functions such as downloading, updating, and, if you have them switched on, iCloud and device backup, are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you choose to send Apple crash and usage reports from your device, that is a setting on your phone and a relationship between you and Apple; it is not something the app switches on or reads.

Children

Micro Rockets is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to appeal primarily to them. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your data, and getting rid of it

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to hand over, correct or erase on request. Everything the app stores is yours and under your control: deleting the app removes what is on the device, and the iCloud copy is removed the way any app's iCloud data is. If you want to keep the app but start again, MENU › Settings › Start over replaces the company with a new one.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Micro Rockets changes what it does with data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
