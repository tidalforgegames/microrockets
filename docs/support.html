Common questions

Do I have to leave the app open for a flight?

No. Flights and voyages run on a real clock, not on the app being in front of you. Close it, lock the phone, come back tomorrow: everything that was due lands while you are away, and the first screen you see says what happened. If you want to watch, Settings › Keep screen on in flight stops the phone dimming mid-launch.

A job says I cannot fly it

The card says which of the four things is missing, and only one of them is money. A job needs a rocket with the reach to get there, enough payload for what it carries, a pad at the right level, and sometimes a crew or a research node. Tap the destination to see the lightest rocket you own that gets there, or the one that comes closest if none does yet. A job that puts down on a surface also needs a rocket with landing legs, which not every design has.

The board is full of work I cannot do

It should never be. At least one job on the board is always something you can fly with what you own right now, whatever your money and whatever your fleet. If you are looking at four you cannot touch, that is a bug and we would like to hear about it with a screenshot.

Nothing seems to be happening

Check the PAD tab first: an idle pad is an idle company. Then FLEET — a ship with no route is parked, and a ship that says it is holding has arrived somewhere with no free berth and is waiting for one, which usually means that base needs another berth or that hull needs a different route.

I got a new phone

Install the app, sign in to the same Apple Account, and open it. A second copy of your company lives in your own iCloud and the newer copy wins, so the new phone picks up where the old one left off. If you had turned Settings › Cloud save off, there is no copy to restore and the company stays on the phone it was on.

I want to see the how-to cards again

Settings › Show plays them again from the first one. They also stop appearing once read, so this is the only way back to them.

The text is too small

Settings › Larger text puts body text one step up on every screen. The game is drawn in a pixel face at fixed sizes, so this is the control rather than the system text size.

Notifications are too much, or not enough

There are three, and they are separate: a flight landing, a ship docking, and research finishing. Turn off the ones you do not want in Settings. They are scheduled on your phone, so turning them off stops them being made at all.

I want to start over

Settings › Reset, then confirm. It gives you a new company from the first job and keeps your preferences on that screen. It cannot be undone, and if cloud save is on the reset company replaces the old one everywhere.

Reporting a bug

Please include the version from Settings › About, what you were doing, and whether it happens every time. A screenshot of the screen that is wrong is worth a great deal. If the app closed unexpectedly, Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Analytics Data on your iPhone contains a file beginning with MicroRockets; attaching it helps.
